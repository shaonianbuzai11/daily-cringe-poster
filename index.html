Daily Cringe Poster

A publishing tool used by the Daily Cringe account owner.

Daily Cringe Poster helps the account owner publish their own short videos to their own accounts, including TikTok (@1_dailycringe) and YouTube (Daily Cringe, @Daily.Cringe1).

The owner signs in with the platform account, authorizes the tool, previews each video, and approves publishing. The tool uploads the approved video file and, where the platform allows it after review, posts it to the owner's profile. It does not collect other users' data, does not sell data, and is not offered to other users at this time.
