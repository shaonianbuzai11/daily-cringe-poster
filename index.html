Product overview

Built for one creator operation

Daily Cringe Poster serves the Daily Cringe short-video operation. The account owner selects videos they own or are authorized to publish, reviews every edit, and approves each post before anything is sent to a platform.

Preview before publish

No video is published automatically without owner review. The owner checks the final video, title, caption, thumbnail, destination account, and visibility before approving an upload or direct post.

Official APIs only

The tool connects through TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API, and through the YouTube Data API. It does not scrape platforms, automate personal browser sessions, or collect other users' content or data.

How it works

1

Connect the owner's account. The owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit and grants only the permissions listed below. The tool verifies that the authorized TikTok creator is @1_dailycringe before any upload.

2

Prepare the video. The owner adds their own short video, checks the edit, and prepares the title, caption, and thumbnail intended for publication.

3

Preview and approve. The owner previews the exact video and publishing details. Publishing stops here unless the owner explicitly approves that specific video.

4

Upload. After approval, the tool uploads the approved video file to the owner's TikTok account using the Content Posting API upload capability.

5

Publish and verify. Where production approval allows, the tool publishes the approved video to the owner's own profile and then checks the resulting status so the owner can record the published link.

Daily Cringe Poster is operated by and for the Daily Cringe account owner. It is not offered as a public posting service for unrelated users at this time.

Trust, privacy, and control

Data minimization

The tool processes the account identifiers needed to verify the owner, plus the video files and publishing metadata the owner approves. It does not sell personal information or build advertising profiles.

Revocable access

The owner can revoke TikTok or YouTube API access from the connected platform's account settings at any time. Revocation stops future API publishing until the owner authorizes the tool again.

Policy access

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are public, linked from every page of this website, and available without signing in.
