Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Daily Cringe Poster is a tool operated by and for the Daily Cringe account owner to publish the owner's own short videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Information we process

When the owner authorizes the tool, the platforms provide access tokens and basic account identifiers (such as open ID, channel/account ID, display name, avatar, and username) needed to confirm the correct account and upload videos. The tool processes video files, titles, and captions that the owner approves for publishing.

How information is used

Information is used only to sign the owner in, verify the authorized account, upload approved videos, check upload/publish status, and record the resulting links for the owner. We do not sell personal information, do not use it for advertising profiling, and do not collect data about other platform users.

Storage and security

Access tokens and client credentials are stored locally in access-controlled files used by the tool and are not published on this website. The owner may revoke access at any time from the connected platform's account settings, after which stored tokens should be deleted on request.

Sharing

Approved videos and metadata are sent to TikTok and/or YouTube only when the owner approves publishing to those platforms. We do not share personal information with other third parties except the platforms the owner chooses to publish to.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to the Daily Cringe account owner through the TikTok (@1_dailycringe) or YouTube (@Daily.Cringe1) profiles.
